# The Kyivstoner plot in Denmark and Moscow's answer: evidence register

Evidence register and data behind the rapid assessment published by the [Disinformation Commission](https://disinformationcommission.com) on 21 November 2026.

On 20 November 2026 Denmark's security service said Russian intelligence planned the alleged September plot to kill the rapper Kyivstoner near Copenhagen. The assessment measures how Moscow answered, how its line reached Danish readers through the Pravda Danmark copy site, and what Russia's hired proxies are paid in comparable European cases.

- Full investigation and charts: [disinformationcommission.com/investigation/denmark-kyivstoner-plot](https://disinformationcommission.com/investigation/denmark-kyivstoner-plot)
- Report PDF and permanent DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.24429537](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24429537)

- `denmark-kyivstoner-evidence.csv`: the evidence register, one row per source.
